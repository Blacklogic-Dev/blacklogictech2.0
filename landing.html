BlackLogic Infrastructure Solutions

BlackLogic Technologies is a South African digital infrastructure engineering company based in Middelburg, Mpumalanga. Through four specialised divisions, we deliver business consultation, visual design, media production and digital systems engineering for organisations across South Africa and Africa.
