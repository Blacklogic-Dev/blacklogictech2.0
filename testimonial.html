Digital Occurrence Book

Operational Cloud System

01

The Problem

Operational occurrence records and reporting workflows required a more structured digital environment. The challenge was to transform the traditional occurrence-book workflow into a cloud-based operational system while maintaining usability, accountability and secure access.

The Engineering Response

BlackLogic engineered a digital Occurrence Book environment combining authentication, cloud data storage, structured reporting, audit trails, search and filtering capabilities and a dedicated operational vault.

The 24-Hour Engineering Cycle

  • Problem analysis and workflow understanding
  • System architecture and interface planning
  • UX/UI implementation
  • Firebase authentication and database integration
  • Testing, failure identification and resolution
  • Cloud deployment and operational presentation

Engineering Outcome

The result was a working cloud-based operational system designed to digitise occurrence recording and improve visibility over operational events and records.
